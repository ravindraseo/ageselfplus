What Is SelfPlus AGE+?

SelfPlus AGE+ is a dietary supplement designed to be a small, easy addition to a healthy-aging routine. Each capsule supplies 125 mg of trans-resveratrol, drawn from Japanese knotweed root extract that is standardized to 50%. A bottle holds 30 capsules — about one month at the labeled serving of one capsule per day.

Resveratrol is a polyphenol: a protective compound plants make when they're stressed by UV light, injury or fungi. People know it best from red grapes and red wine, but those foods contain only tiny amounts. Japanese knotweed root is far richer, which is why it's the go-to source for most resveratrol supplements, AGE+ included.

It's important to be clear about what AGE+ is not. It isn't a medicine and it isn't a shortcut. It's a nutritional supplement meant to complement — never replace — balanced meals, physical activity, rest and the advice of your healthcare provider.

How Does SelfPlus AGE+ Work?

The idea behind AGE+ is straightforward: deliver a consistent, measured amount of trans-resveratrol every day. Trans-resveratrol is the stable, naturally dominant form of the compound and the version used in most human studies, which is why the label names it specifically.

After you swallow a capsule, resveratrol is absorbed through the gut and then processed quickly by the intestines and liver. Because the body clears it fairly fast, steady daily use tends to make more sense than taking it now and then — and pairing it with a meal that contains a little fat may help, since resveratrol dissolves better in fat than in water.

What researchers have looked at

Scientists have studied resveratrol for decades. In laboratory and animal research it has been examined for its relationship with oxidative stress, cellular signaling (including proteins called sirtuins that are involved in cellular upkeep), metabolic markers such as glucose handling, and blood-vessel function.

Human studies are more mixed. Some trials report modest changes in certain markers; others find little difference. Results seem to depend on the dose, the length of the study and the people taking part. In other words, the research is genuinely interesting, but it does not prove that any resveratrol supplement prevents disease, reverses aging or extends lifespan. If you'd like to read more from an independent source, the Linus Pauling Institute's resveratrol overview is a balanced place to start.

The honest takeaway: AGE+ works as a convenient, consistent source of a well-researched plant compound. How it fits into your own routine is something you judge over weeks of regular use — which is exactly what the 60-day refund window allows.

Resveratrol from food vs. a supplement

A common question is whether you can simply get resveratrol from your diet. You do get some: red and purple grapes, blueberries, cranberries, mulberries, peanuts, pistachios and dark chocolate all contain it. The catch is quantity. A typical serving of these foods provides well under one milligram, and a glass of red wine usually contains only a couple of milligrams at most — along with alcohol you probably don't want more of.

That's the gap a standardized knotweed extract fills. One AGE+ capsule supplies 125 mg in a measured, repeatable amount. Keep enjoying colorful fruits, nuts and vegetables for their fiber, vitamins and flavor; think of AGE+ as a convenient way to add a consistent amount of one specific compound on top of that foundation.

SelfPlus AGE+ Ingredients

AGE+ keeps its formula deliberately short. The active part of every capsule comes from one botanical source, and the label tells you exactly how much of the key compound you're getting.

✔ Japanese Knotweed Root (Polygonum cuspidatum)

Japanese knotweed is a hardy, fast-growing perennial plant native to East Asia. Its roots have a long history in traditional herbal practice and are one of the richest natural sources of resveratrol ever identified.

That concentration is what makes knotweed practical for supplements: it allows a meaningful amount of resveratrol to fit into a single, normal-sized capsule — something that simply isn't possible with grape skins or wine.

✔ Trans-Resveratrol (125 mg per capsule, 50% standardized)

Trans-resveratrol is the featured compound in AGE+. "Trans" refers to the molecule's shape; it's the more stable form and the one most frequently used in human research, which is why quality labels name it specifically rather than just saying "resveratrol."

"Standardized to 50%" means the extract is tested so that half its weight is trans-resveratrol. Standardization helps keep the amount you get consistent from one batch — and one bottle — to the next.

Inactive ingredients such as the capsule shell are listed under "Other ingredients" on the Supplement Facts panel. Always check the bottle you receive, as formulations can be updated.
